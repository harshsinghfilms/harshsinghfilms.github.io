About

I’m Harsh Singh, a writer, director, and animator focused on developing original films and visual stories. My work sits at the intersection of storytelling, filmmaking, animation, and visual world-building, with a particular interest in creating immersive worlds, complex characters, and narratives driven by human choices.

I approach filmmaking from both creative and technical perspectives. Writing allows me to develop the story, characters, themes, and structure; directing allows me to shape those ideas into a cohesive cinematic experience; and animation and visual effects give me the ability to explore and build worlds that may otherwise be difficult to bring to the screen.

I’m particularly drawn to stories that place characters under pressure and examine how people respond when their relationships, beliefs, and responsibilities are tested. I believe strong visual storytelling should serve the story rather than exist simply for spectacle, and I aim to combine ambitious visual ideas with grounded emotional experiences.

Alongside developing my own films and screenplays, I continue to build my skills across writing, directing, animation, visual development, and production. My long-term goal is to develop and produce original feature films with a distinct visual identity, strong narrative foundations, and the scale to create genuinely immersive cinematic experiences.

This portfolio is a collection of the work I’ve created and the stories I’m developing along the way.
